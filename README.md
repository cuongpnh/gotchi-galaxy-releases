# Gotchi Galaxy

A pixel-art pocket pet you raise, breed and take to far-away planets. Free, no account, no ads.

## Choose your way to play

1. **Mac** (the full game, plays with friends on the same Wi-Fi)
   - Download the Mac app: [Apple Silicon (M1 and later)](https://github.com/cuongpnh/gotchi-galaxy-releases/releases/download/v0.8.33/gotchi-galaxy-0.8.33-arm64-mac.zip) or [Intel](https://github.com/cuongpnh/gotchi-galaxy-releases/releases/download/v0.8.33/gotchi-galaxy-0.8.33-x64-mac.zip).
   - Unzip it and drag **Gotchi Galaxy** to Applications.
   - Open it. The first time, your Mac asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: choose **Open**. After that it updates itself.
2. **Android** (the full game, plays with friends on the same Wi-Fi)
   - [Download the app](https://github.com/cuongpnh/gotchi-galaxy-releases/releases/download/v0.8.36/gotchi-galaxy-0.8.36-android.apk) on your phone and open it.
   - Android asks to allow installs from this source: say yes once.
   - New versions are offered inside the game, in Settings.
3. **iPhone app** through TestFlight (the full game, plays with friends on the same Wi-Fi)
   - Install Apple's **TestFlight** app from the App Store.
   - On your iPhone, open [the Gotchi Galaxy invite](https://testflight.apple.com/join/AsjBScbr), tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
   - New versions arrive in TestFlight; it offers each one.
4. **iPhone app** through SideStore, without TestFlight (the full game; about 15 minutes the first time, and a computer for the setup)
   - Follow the [step-by-step iPhone guide](https://cuongpnh.github.io/gotchi-galaxy-releases/ios/).
5. **iPhone web version** (one link, solo play)
   - Open [the web version](https://cuongpnh.github.io/gotchi-galaxy-releases/play/) in Safari, then Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - Playing with friends needs the app. The web version is solo play, and your save lives in that browser.

## Install on iPhone with SideStore

SideStore is a free app that installs apps like Gotchi Galaxy on your iPhone with your own Apple ID, no App Store needed. You do the setup once.

**What you need**
- An iPhone
- A computer (Mac or Windows), for the first setup only
- Your Apple ID (a free one is fine)
- About 15 minutes

**Steps**
1. On your computer, open [sidestore.io](https://sidestore.io) and follow its install guide for your computer and iPhone. This puts SideStore itself on your phone. Follow their steps as they show them: they know best.
2. Open SideStore on your iPhone and check that it is signed in with your Apple ID.
3. On your iPhone, tap this link to add our source: [Add the Gotchi Galaxy source](sidestore://source?url=https://cuongpnh.github.io/gotchi-galaxy-releases/altstore.json). SideStore opens and asks you to confirm.
4. If the link did nothing, add the source by hand: in SideStore, add a new source and paste this address: `https://cuongpnh.github.io/gotchi-galaxy-releases/altstore.json`
5. Find **Gotchi Galaxy** in the source and install it. Wait until it finishes.
6. If iOS says the developer is not trusted when you open the game: Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then **Trust**.
7. If iOS asks for Developer Mode: Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode, switch it on, and let the iPhone restart.
8. Open Gotchi Galaxy and play.

**Keeping it working**
- An app installed this way works for 7 days, then needs a refresh. SideStore refreshes it for you in the background, so open SideStore now and then and leave it installed.
- When a new version comes out, the game says **Update in SideStore** in Settings. Tap it, and SideStore opens.

**If something goes wrong**
- *The game will not open, or closes at once:* its 7 days ran out. Open SideStore, refresh Gotchi Galaxy, and try again.
- *"3 app limit" or "too many apps":* a free Apple ID can keep only 3 apps installed this way. Remove one you do not use, then install again.
- *The source link did nothing:* add the source by hand (step 4).
- *SideStore itself will not install or refresh:* its own help pages at [sidestore.io](https://sidestore.io) cover that.

Playing with friends needs this app version. The [web version](https://cuongpnh.github.io/gotchi-galaxy-releases/play/) is solo play.
