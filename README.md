# Gotchi Galaxy

A pixel-art pocket pet you raise, breed and take to far-away planets. Free, no account, no ads.

## Choose your way to play

1. **Mac** (the full game, plays with friends on the same Wi-Fi)
   - Download the Mac app: [Apple Silicon (M1 and later)](https://github.com/cuongpnh/gotchi-galaxy-releases/releases/download/v0.8.54/gotchi-galaxy-0.8.54-arm64-mac.zip) or [Intel](https://github.com/cuongpnh/gotchi-galaxy-releases/releases/download/v0.8.54/gotchi-galaxy-0.8.54-x64-mac.zip).
   - Unzip it and drag **Gotchi Galaxy** to Applications.
   - Open it. The first time, your Mac asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: choose **Open**. After that it updates itself.
2. **Android** (the full game, plays with friends on the same Wi-Fi)
   - [Download the app](https://github.com/cuongpnh/gotchi-galaxy-releases/releases/download/v0.8.50/gotchi-galaxy-0.8.50-android.apk) on your phone and open it.
   - Android asks to allow installs from this source: say yes once.
   - New versions are offered inside the game, in Settings.
3. **iPhone app** through TestFlight (the full game, plays with friends on the same Wi-Fi)
   - Install Apple's **TestFlight** app from the App Store.
   - On your iPhone, open [the Gotchi Galaxy invite](https://testflight.apple.com/join/AsjBScbr), tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
   - New versions arrive in TestFlight; it offers each one.
4. **Web version** (one link, any phone or computer)
   - Open [the web version](https://cuongpnh.github.io/gotchi-galaxy-releases/play/), and on an iPhone use Safari, then Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - It plays online with friends too (the square, friends, boards). Only playing on the same Wi-Fi with nearby players needs an app.
   - Your save lives in that browser.
